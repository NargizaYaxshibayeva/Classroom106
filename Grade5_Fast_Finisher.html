Fast Finisher Challenge

Grade 5 · B1/B1+ stretch questions · 10 scenario-based MCQs · 10–15 minutes

Challenge level: B1 / B1+

Think about the situation

These questions are a little more difficult than the main test. Read each situation, think about what the speaker means, and choose the most natural and grammatically correct response.

When you finish, select Check answers. You’ll see your score, why the answer is best, and a grammar or vocabulary reminder.

This is an offline, rule-based checker. It checks the selected multiple-choice answer and gives prepared feedback; it does not use a live AI service.
