3. Write sentences

Write your own sentences using Unit 3 grammar. The checker looks for common grammar patterns and gives advice. It is an automatic rule-based checker, so it may not recognise every correct sentence or understand every meaning perfectly.

4. Grammar multiple-choice quiz

This quiz revises countable and uncountable nouns, a/an/some/any, how much/how many, too/enough, and food descriptions.
